B.3 · The fit rule (replaces h·2/3 ≤ minPitch − 8/3)
One factor per level, computed in the 390×844 board space before the board scales to the screen, and applied to every doll so the sizes keep their ratios:
k = min(1, min(V, HUD, H) / heightOf(maxSize))
V   = (pitchY − 40 − 12.7 − 2) / 0.9661
HUD = (planksY[0] − 12.7 − 150) / 0.9661
H   = (min(stand, pitch) − 8) / 0.5353
0.9661 is the drawn art box above the base (742/768), which covers every skin's topper (Space's antenna reaches art y 1). 0.5353 is the widest painted part of every skin (411 of 512 px) × 2/3. 150 is the family counter's bottom (142) + 8. The old rule was a width test on the full art box, which is 20 % wider than any doll; the real limit on three shelves is the plank above. The picked doll keeps its 14 dp lift and may pass in front of the plank above while it is lifted.
